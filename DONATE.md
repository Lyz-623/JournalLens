## Where Support Goes / 支持会用在哪里

Financial support helps with maintenance work that is easy to overlook: testing against Zotero releases, adapting to publisher page changes, fixing journal parsing issues, improving figure extraction, and preparing releases.

维护工作往往不显眼，但很耗时间：适配 Zotero 新版本、应对出版商页面变化、修复期刊解析问题、优化 Figure 抓取，以及准备发布包。

Thank you for using JournalLens.

感谢你使用 JournalLens。
